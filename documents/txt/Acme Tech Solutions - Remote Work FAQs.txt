Acme Tech Solutions: Remote Work
FAQs
This document provides answers to common questions regarding the Acme Tech Solutions
Remote Work Policy. If you have a question not covered here, please consult the full policy
document or contact your HR Business Partner.
Eligibility and Application
Q: Can anyone at Acme Tech Solutions work remotely?
A: Not automatically. Eligibility depends on the nature of your role, your performance record,
and the operational needs of your team. Employees currently on Performance Improvement
Plans (PIPs) are generally not eligible. All remote work arrangements must be formally
approved.
Q: How do I apply to work remotely or switch to a hybrid schedule?
A: You must submit a formal request through the HR portal (Workday) at least four (4) weeks
before your desired start date. Your direct manager will then schedule a meeting to discuss it.
Final approval requires sign-off from both your manager and the Department Head.
Equipment and Expenses
Q: Does the company pay for my home internet?
A: Acme Tech Solutions provides a monthly stipend of $50 to help subsidize the cost of your
high-speed internet connection.
Q: What equipment will the company provide for my home office?
A: The company will provide one (1) standard company-issued laptop and necessary software
licenses (like VPN and endpoint protection).
Q: Can I get a monitor or an ergonomic chair for my home office?
A: Yes, you can request additional equipment (such as a monitor, keyboard, or ergonomic chair)
up to a maximum value of $500. This request is subject to approval by your manager. Please
note that all purchased equipment remains the property of Acme Tech Solutions.
Daily Operations and Hours
Q: Do I have to work specific hours if I am remote?
A: Yes. While remote work offers flexibility, Acme Tech Solutions has established "Core Hours"
from 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM (Local Time). All employees must be online and available for
meetings and synchronous communication during this window.
Q: I am an hourly (non-exempt) employee. How do I handle overtime while remote?
A: You must accurately track all your hours using Kronos. If you need to work overtime, it must
be pre-approved in writing by your direct manager. Working off-the-clock is strictly prohibited.
Q: Do I have to keep my camera on during meetings?
A: While not strictly mandatory for every single interaction, employees are strongly
encouraged to keep their cameras on during internal and external video conferences to foster
engagement and team connection.
Security and Environment
Q: Can I work from a local coffee shop?
A: Yes, but you must prioritize data security. You are strictly prohibited from using unsecured
public Wi-Fi to access sensitive company data. If you work in a public space, you must use a
secure mobile hotspot or the company VPN. You must also ensure your screen is not visible to
unauthorized individuals.
Q: What should I do if I suspect my company laptop was compromised while traveling?
A: You must report any suspected security breach, data loss, or unauthorized access to the
Information Security Officer (Infosec@acmetech.com) immediately, within 1 hour of discovery.
Performance
Q: What happens if I struggle to hit my goals while working remotely?
A: Performance expectations remain the same whether you are in the office or remote. If your
remote setup negatively impacts your performance or team dynamics, the company reserves
the right to modify or terminate your remote work agreement. You would be given a minimum
of two (2) weeks' notice to return to the office.
